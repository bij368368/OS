OUR STORY

It started with a samosa.

Nothing dramatic. No grand business plan. We just really like samosas.

And we couldn't understand why something so brilliant had been given so little attention. Same fillings. Same choices. Same sad little box at the back of the freezer.

Surely the triangle deserved more.

So we started messing with it.

We took the small, crispy Gujarati-style samosas we grew up with and started wondering what else we could put inside them.

Paneer Makhani? Obviously. Mexican Street Corn? Go on then. Falafel & Zhoug? Absolutely. Truffle Mac & Cheese? That one probably shouldn't work. It really does.

Before long, our kitchen had become a slightly chaotic tour of world street food — with everything eventually ending up inside a triangle.

We're not trying to make samosas fancy.

Quite the opposite. Samosas are already brilliant. We're just giving them a few more stamps in their passport.

Our inspiration comes from the food we love eating: Indian kitchens, street stalls, late-night snacks, family tables and dishes from around the world that make you immediately want another bite.

Then we ask one very important question:
Would that work in a samosa?

Small triangles. Big ideas.

We keep them frozen because that's convenient. We make them for the air fryer because nobody needs a pan of hot oil on a Tuesday night. And we fill them with proper flavour because life's too short for disappointing snacks.

Some are traditional-ish. Some definitely aren't. And Green Chilli, Cheese, Onion & Coriander comes with an EXTRA SPICY warning for a reason.

That's really it.

We're not here to change the world. We'd just like to make your freezer a bit more interesting.

One little triangle at a time.
